How it works

  1. Open YouTube in any tab.
  2. Click the CleanFeed icon (top-right of your browser) and flip on the blockers you want.
  3. If you ever need comments back, a small show comments button appears at the bottom of every video.
  4. For extra tuning, open the Options page — pin specific channels and write custom CSS rules.
